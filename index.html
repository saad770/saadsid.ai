01 About

I work at the intersection of AI strategy, enterprise transformation, and the practical mechanics of getting frontier technology into the hands of the people who run real systems.

My day job is helping enterprises move from AI-curious to AI-native — translating frontier capability into operating models, governance, and the un-glamorous plumbing that actually ships. I spend my time with technology leaders, executive sponsors, and the architects carrying the real weight.

Outside of that, I write. The essays here are an attempt to think clearly about what's coming — the convergence of capital, intelligence, and attention — without the usual hype-cycle compression.

Currently

  • Writing The Inflection Point — a five-part series on the AI convergence.
  • Building internal frameworks for enterprise AI transformation at Microsoft.
  • Exploring the economics of attention and the next platform shift.
  • Based in Austin, working with teams across North America.
