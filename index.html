About

My day job is helping enterprises move from AI-curious to AI-native — translating frontier capability into operating models, governance, and the un-glamorous plumbing that actually ships. I spend my time with technology leaders, executive sponsors, and the architects who carry the actual weight.

Outside of that, I write. The essays here are an attempt to think clearly about what's coming — the convergence of capital, intelligence, and attention — without the usual hype cycle compression.

I also build tooling: a self-contained HTML presentation kit and a Python deck builder that mirrors it, all under the same design tokens you see on this site. It's open and on GitHub.
