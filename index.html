About

I read more than I write. I write more than I publish. Most of what I think about lives at the edge of attention, intimacy, agency, and faith — the older questions that machines are now quietly forcing back open.

Some of it is about love, family, and meaning. Some is about markets, AI, and the long arc of what we are becoming. None of it resolves cleanly, which is part of why it's worth circling.

I also have a day job in AI strategy at Microsoft. It's a real job and I like it. But it's the smallest part of the equation, and not the part this site is for.
