The “1 in 7” fact-check

Verified replacement

The widely-cited claim that “1 in 7 (14%) U.S. women aged 18–24 are on OnlyFans” is a viral extrapolation — OnlyFans has never released age-disaggregated creator data.

The most defensible primary-sourced figure is roughly ~2% of U.S. women aged 18–45 as OF creators. The 8–14% range for 18–24 is a derived estimate — directionally plausible but not primary-sourced.
